# Licensing FAQ

## Is internal SaaS infrastructure considered internal use?
No. If the infrastructure supports a revenue-generating service,
it is considered commercial use.

## What if customers never see the node?
Visibility is irrelevant. Revenue enablement constitutes commercial use.

## What if we are a startup?
Startups are commercial entities and require a commercial license.

## What if we only use one workflow?
Scope does not change license requirements.

## Do proof-of-concepts require a license?
Internal, non-customer-facing POCs are permitted.
Production SaaS deployments require a license.

## Can I modify the code?
Yes. You may modify the code for any permitted use case.
However, modified versions remain subject to the same license terms.

## Can I contribute improvements?
Yes. Contributions are welcome via pull requests.
All contributions are licensed under the same BSL 1.1 terms.

## What happens after the Change Date?
On January 1, 2030, the code becomes available under the Apache License 2.0.

## How do I obtain a commercial license?
Contact licensing@velobpa.com for commercial licensing inquiries.

## What does a commercial license cost?
Commercial license pricing varies based on organization size, deployment scope,
and support requirements. Contact us for a quote.
